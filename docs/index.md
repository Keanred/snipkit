# Snippetry Support

Snippetry is a macOS snippet manager that keeps your snippets, tags, and folders on your own device and in your own iCloud account.

Need help, found a bug, or have a feature request? We're happy to help.

## Contact

Email us at **[snippetryapp@gmail.com](mailto:snippetryapp@gmail.com)** and we'll get back to you as soon as we can. When reporting a problem, it helps to include your macOS version and a short description of what you were doing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where are my snippets stored?

On your Mac, in the app's local storage, and — if you're signed into iCloud with iCloud Drive enabled for Snippetry — synced to your personal iCloud account. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) for details.

### Can the developer see my snippets?

No. Snippetry has no analytics, tracking, or third-party services. Your data lives on your device and in your private iCloud account, which the developer cannot access. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).

### My snippets aren't syncing between devices. What can I do?

Make sure each device is signed into the **same Apple ID**, has **iCloud Drive** enabled, and has Snippetry turned on in iCloud settings. Syncing can take a moment and requires a network connection. If it still doesn't work, email us at [snippetryapp@gmail.com](mailto:snippetryapp@gmail.com).

### How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email **[snippetryapp@gmail.com](mailto:snippetryapp@gmail.com)** with a description of the issue or idea. For bugs, please include your macOS version and the steps to reproduce the problem.

### How do I back up or export my data?

Your snippets sync to your private iCloud account automatically when iCloud is enabled, so they are available on any device signed into the same Apple ID.

## Privacy

Snippetry does not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your data. Read the full [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).
